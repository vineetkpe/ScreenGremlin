# Interaction benchmark notes

Observed patterns worth matching without copying third-party art or IP:

- Desktop-mascot products feel better when the character perches on edges/title bars and common utilities are directly available from the character.
- Shimeji-style pets feel alive because of climbing/hanging, many animation states, cursor reactions, drag/drop, and physics.
- Stronger original desktop pets use many hand-authored poses and avoid random teleporting as the primary movement model.

v1.3 applies those interaction patterns with original ScreenGremlin character art, exact park/dangle placement, rope visuals, cursor/hover reactions, and direct local productivity actions.
