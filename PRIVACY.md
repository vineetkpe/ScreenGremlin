# ScreenGremlin Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

ScreenGremlin is designed to work locally on your computer.

## Data ScreenGremlin stores

The desktop app stores its settings and, if activated, its Pro license key in the Electron application user-data directory on your computer.

## Data ScreenGremlin does not collect

The app does not include analytics, telemetry, advertising SDKs, an account system, or background usage tracking.

ScreenGremlin does not read the content of your files, browser tabs, messages, or documents as part of its normal operation.

## External links

The app may open a checkout, download, or creator-unlock webpage when you explicitly click the corresponding button. Those external services have their own privacy practices.

## Changes

If future versions add network services or analytics, this policy should be updated before those features are released.
